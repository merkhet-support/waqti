The name

Our studio, Merkhet, is named after one of the oldest timekeeping tools we know. More than 3,000 years ago, Egyptian astronomers held a weighted line against the North Star to tell the hours of the night. People have always kept time by the sky — by the dawn, the sunset and the stars. We just put it in your pocket.
