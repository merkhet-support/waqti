Waqti terms of use

These terms are between you and Merkhet ("we"), the studio that makes Waqti. By using Waqti you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

1. What Waqti is

Waqti helps you plan habits, tasks, prayers, fasts and reminders on your own phone. It works without an account. Your data stays on your device and in your phone's own backup; see the privacy policy.

2. Reminders can fail

Phones can delay or block notifications (battery savers, "do not disturb", permissions, updates). Waqti does its best but can't promise that every reminder arrives on time. Don't rely on Waqti alone for anything where a missed reminder could cause harm, such as medicine, payments, legal or official deadlines.

3. Not medical, financial or religious advice

  • Waqti doesn't give medical advice. Medicine reminders, the period tracker, quit trackers and health notes are tools, not diagnosis or treatment. The period tracker's dates are estimates and must not be used for contraception. Ask a doctor or pharmacist about your health and medicines.
  • Bill and renewal reminders are only reminders. Waqti never pays anything, connects to a bank or checks official deadlines.
  • Prayer times, Hijri dates and fasting calendars are calculated on your phone and can differ from your mosque, church, synagogue or temple. Follow your local religious authority.

4. Waqti Pro (when available)

Waqti Pro is optional. It's sold through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment, renewal and refunds under their own terms. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your store account settings, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Prices are shown in the store before you buy. Removing the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

5. Sharing with others

Requests and family lists travel as links you choose to send. Anyone holding a list's invite link can read and join that list, so send it only to people you trust. Don't use Waqti to send spam, harass anyone, or impersonate someone else. You can block anyone from Settings → Requests & people.

6. Your content

What you write in Waqti is yours. We don't receive it, read it or sell it.

7. Age

Waqti is meant for people aged 13 and older. Younger users should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.

8. Our responsibility

Waqti is provided "as is". As far as the law allows, we aren't responsible for losses caused by a missed or late reminder, a calculation that differs from your local practice, or lost data (keep a backup). Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer protection law.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. If a change matters, we'll say so in the app's "What's new".

10. Law and contact

These terms are governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt. Questions: merkhet.support@gmail.com.
